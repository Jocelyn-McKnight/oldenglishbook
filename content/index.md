Welcome to **The New Old English Textbook**, an **open educational resource (OER)** designed for university courses. This textbook provides a comprehensive yet accessible introduction to the Old English language, covering grammar, vocabulary, and historical context.

Old English, spoken and written between approximately 450 and 1100 CE, is a deeply inflected language with a rich literary tradition. Understanding Old English not only enhances linguistic knowledge but also provides insight into the cultural and intellectual history of early medieval England.

As an open resource, this textbook is freely available for use, adaptation, and distribution. With lessons, sources (primary and secondary), and exercises, it serves as both a learning tool and a reference guide.
